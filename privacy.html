Matrix Monitor – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Matrix Monitor is a private, internal tool used only by its operator for TikTok accounts the operator owns.

Data we read

With the account's permission, the tool reads: basic profile information (display name, username), profile statistics (follower, following, like and video counts) and the public video list with its view, like, comment and share counts.

How data is used and stored

The data is used only to show the operator follower growth and post performance. It is stored on the operator's own computer, is not sold, and is not shared with any third party.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the account remains connected. Removing Matrix Monitor in the TikTok app settings revokes access; stored data can be deleted by the operator at any time.

Contact

Questions: contact the operator at the email address registered with this app.
