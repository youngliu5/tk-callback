Matrix Monitor – Terms of Service

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Matrix Monitor is a private, internal tool. It is used only by its operator to view statistics of TikTok accounts that the operator owns and manages.

Use

Access is limited to the operator's own accounts, each of which authorizes the tool through TikTok Login. The tool only reads profile statistics and the public video list. It does not post, edit, delete or message on any account.

Revoking access

Any connected account can remove Matrix Monitor at any time in the TikTok app settings under authorized apps. Access ends immediately.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is, for internal reporting only.

Contact

Questions: contact the operator at the email address registered with this app.
